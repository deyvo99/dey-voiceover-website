# Opening welcome recording

Suggested length: 20–25 seconds. Read it like the opening of a beloved stage show: intimate at first, then brighter as the curtain rises.

## Main version

> Hello, traveler. I’m Da Eun.
>
> Every good story begins with a voice—sometimes a whisper, sometimes a spark, and sometimes one brave little question: what if?
>
> Wherever you came from, I’m so glad you found your way here.
>
> The curtain is rising. Choose a world, press play… and let’s begin.

## Optional bilingual ending

Replace the final sentence with:

> The curtain is rising. 자, 우리 이야기를 시작해 볼까요? Let’s begin.

## Recording notes

- Leave about half a second of quiet before the first word.
- Start warm and close, as if telling one person a secret.
- Let “what if?” feel playful and full of possibility.
- Brighten slightly from “Wherever you came from…” onward.
- Pause briefly after “press play” before the final words.
- Export as MP3 and save it as `assets/audio/opening-welcome.mp3`.
