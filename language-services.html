✦The first · Written language✦

Translation that sounds written — not translated.

I translate between Korean and English with attention to meaning, rhythm, audience, and tone. The goal is not a word-for-word mirror; it is a version that feels clear and natural in the language your reader actually receives.

How we find the right words.

    Share the context

  2. II

    Confirm the scope

What it costs

Translation is quoted per word or per project; interpretation is quoted by the hour or by the day. Subject matter, turnaround, and whether you need certification all affect the figure.

  • Translation: per word or project
  • Interpretation: hourly or daily
  • Quotes are free

Every project is reviewed before booking. Certified, legal, medical, highly technical, or simultaneous interpretation requirements must be discussed and explicitly confirmed in advance.
