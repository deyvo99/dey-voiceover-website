✦Why audio?✦

Meet your readers where they’re listening.

An audiobook gives your story another way to be discovered, experienced, and remembered.

I

Reach listening readers

Connect with people who experience books while commuting, walking, resting, or working.

II

Make your story accessible

Offer another way into your book for readers who prefer or rely on audio.

III

Let emotion breathe

Voice can reveal rhythm, character, humor, tension, and feeling that live between the lines.

IV

Grow your catalogue

Add a new format to the work you have already created and expand how readers can find it.

✦From manuscript to microphone✦

How your audiobook comes to life.

  1. I

    Tell me about your book

    We begin with genre, word count, characters, tone, timeline, and your goals for the finished audio.

  2. II

    Find the voice

    A sample helps us agree on narration style, character approach, pronunciation, and overall feeling.

  3. III

    Record the story

    Once the direction is approved, I record the manuscript in my professional home studio.

  4. IV

    Choose your finish

    Receive organized narration files for your own engineer, or choose a full production coordinated with an audio engineer.

  5. V

    Prepare for listeners

    We complete the agreed review and delivery process so you know exactly what files you are receiving.

What it costs

Narration is quoted per finished hour — the length of the finished audiobook, not the hours spent recording it. What moves the figure: total running time, whether you want narration only or a full production with engineering, how much character work the book asks for, and how soon you need it.

  • Quoted per finished hour
  • Narration only, or full production
  • Quotes are free

Every book is different. Pricing, schedule, revision rounds, engineering scope and final technical specifications are all confirmed in writing before recording begins.
