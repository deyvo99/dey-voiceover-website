02 The storyteller

Once upon a time…

I didn’t just find
my voice. I followed it.

SeoulThe beginning: cassette stories, puppet kingdoms, a very big imagination.
ChicagoTheatre at Northwestern. Learning how a room can hold its breath.
New YorkPerformer, playwright—and the first voice-over workshop.
SingaporeLanguages, translation, and a new love for connecting worlds.
Now: KoreaBack in Seoul, bringing every chapter into the booth.

My first favorite narrator came from a cassette player in my childhood bedroom. Listening to Percy Jackson in Korea opened a door: to English, to faraway places, and to whole worlds that existed behind closed eyes.

That magic never really left. I’ve carried it through theatre school, New York stages, Singapore trade expos, language classrooms, and now into my home studio. Each chapter taught me something different about listening—and about making someone feel seen.
