✦Chapter Three✦

A letter, left open on the desk

To the author, the publisher, the casting director —

Every book I have narrated began as someone else’s quiet, stubborn work. Long before there was a cover, or a release date, or a page on Audible, there was a person who kept going.

I would very much like the next story to be yours.

If you have a manuscript ready for a voice, I record and produce full audiobooks from my own studio. If it is not ready yet, I also read as an alpha and beta reader — an honest ear before you commit. And if your story needs to cross between Korean and English, I translate and interpret between them professionally.

Tell me what you are making, who it is for, and how you want it to feel. That is always the best place to begin.

Warmly,Da Eun Yoon
